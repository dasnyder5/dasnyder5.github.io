### Princeton University
**MAE 345 / 549 -- [Introduction to Robotics](https://irom-lab.princeton.edu/intro-to-robotics/): Fall 2021**  
_[Professor Anirudha Majumdar](https://mae.princeton.edu/people/faculty/majumdar)_  
An introduction to the basic theoretical and algorithmic principles behind robotic systems, with project-based assignments on quadrotors. Topics include motion planning, control, localization, mapping, and vision.  __Recipient: Crocco Award for Excellence in Teaching__

**MAE 206 -- [Introduction to Engineering Dynamics](https://registrar.princeton.edu/course-offerings/course-details?term=1234&courseid=002322): Spring 2021; Spring 2022**  
_[Professor Anirudha Majumdar](https://mae.princeton.edu/people/faculty/majumdar)_  
Formulation and solution of equations governing the dynamic behavior of engineering systems. Primarily focused on rigid-body Newtonian mechanics.

### University of Maryland, College Park
**ENAE 432 -- [Control of Aerospace Systems](https://aero.umd.edu/course-schedule/course/ENAE432): Spring 2018**  
_[Professor Robert Sanner](https://aero.umd.edu/clark/faculty/63/Robert-M-Sanner)_  
An introduction to the feedback control of dynamic systems.
Laplace transforms and transfer function techniques; frequency
response and Bode diagrams. Stability analysis via root locus and
Nyquist techniques. Performance specifications in time and
frequency domains, and design of compensation strategies to meet
performance goals. 

**FIRE 198 -- First-year Individual Research Experience: Spring 2016**  
_[Professor Derek Paley](https://cdcl.umd.edu/#director), [Dr. Derrick Yeo](https://robotics.umich.edu/people/faculty/derrick-yeo/)_  
A course for individualized research projects for first-year undergraduates in engineering, focused on autonomous unmanned systems (AUS). Included projects in computer vision, autonomous navigation, and human-robot interaction.
